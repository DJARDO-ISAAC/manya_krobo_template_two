Community24 February 2025

Notice2024

Notice of dormant accounts

Accounts not used for five years have been listed as dormant. Reactivate yours at any branch before they are transferred to the Bank of Ghana.

Notice1 March 2021

Sanctions for issuing dud cheques

Issuing a cheque without enough money in the account is a criminal offence punishable by up to five years in prison.

New branch2019

Ashale Botwe branch now open

Our newest branch, at Abudankade Junction, serves Ashale Botwe, Adenta, Madina and the surrounding areas.

Community25 August 2018

40 years of successful banking

At 40, the bank had grown from one branch to eleven, and marked the milestone with gifts to health centres, a free health screening, a football gala and a grand durbar.

Notice2 May 2018

Notice of the 37th Annual General Meeting

Report28 March 2018

Award2018
