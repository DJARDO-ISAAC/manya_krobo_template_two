Our story

Started by women, owned by the community

In 1978 the Krobo Women Association founded a community bank at Abanse, in the Manya Krobo district of the Eastern Region. It was the fifth rural bank licensed in Ghana.

Today we lend to farmers, fishermen, traders and small businesses, and much of our lending still goes to women. Our profits go back into the community, through scholarships, health centres and schools.

Banking safely

We will never ask for your PIN

Not by phone, SMS, WhatsApp or email. If someone asks for your PIN, password or a code sent to your phone, it is a fraud. Hang up and call us on +233 50 822 2743.
